Privacy

Circuit Quest collects nothing. It's a static page that runs entirely in your browser — there is no server, no account, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind.

Your level progress and star counts are saved only in your browser's own localStorage, on your device. That data never gets sent anywhere, is never seen by us, and stays local to whichever browser you're using — clearing your browser's site data erases it permanently, and we have no copy.

The one outside connection the page makes is to Google Fonts, to load the pixel-style typeface — that request goes directly from your browser to Google, the same as visiting any site that uses a Google font.
